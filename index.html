Privacy Policy

Lessen Black · effective 10 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you interact with Lessen Black's official pages and with the advertising we run to promote our music. We are an independent band; we do not operate our own servers, we do not run a shop with customer accounts, and we do not sell or rent personal data to anyone.

1. Who we are

Lessen Black is a melodic death metal band based in Athens, Greece. For anything in this policy, we can be contacted at lessenblack@gmail.com.

2. Advertising on Meta platforms

We promote our releases through paid advertising on Facebook and Instagram using Meta's Marketing API. When you see one of our ads:

  • Meta decides which of its own users are shown the ad, based on the audience criteria we select (for example: age range, country, and interests such as metal music). We do not receive a list of the individuals who were shown an ad.
  • We receive aggregated, anonymised performance statistics from Meta — counts and averages such as impressions, video views, reach and cost. These cannot be used to identify any individual person.
  • We do not upload customer lists, email addresses or phone numbers to create advertising audiences.
  • We may create video-viewer audiences using Meta's own tools. These are groups defined inside Meta's systems (for example "people who watched at least 50% of our video in the last 30 days") so that people who already showed interest in our music may see a later post about the same release. The underlying identity data stays with Meta; we never receive or store it, and we cannot see who is in such a group.

How Meta itself collects and processes data is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy, which we encourage you to read. You can control the ads you see through Meta's Ad Preferences settings, and you can opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies via the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance.

3. Streaming and pre-save platforms

Links we share to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube or pre-save/smart-link pages are operated by those third parties. When you follow such a link, their own privacy policies apply to any data they collect. We receive only aggregate play and follower counts from the analytics dashboards those services provide to artists.

4. Contacting us

If you email us at lessenblack@gmail.com, we keep your message and your address only as long as needed to reply to you or to keep a record of our correspondence. We never add addresses to a marketing list without telling you, and we never pass them to third parties.

5. Data we hold

We maintain no database of fans, no cookies of our own and no tracking pixels on any website we control. Anything Meta holds about your interaction with our ads is held by Meta under its own policies.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of personal data, and to object to certain processing. Because we do not hold personal data about you beyond ordinary email correspondence, most such requests concern data held by Meta and are best directed to Meta. If you would like to exercise a right against us, write to lessenblack@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

7. Changes to this policy

If our practices change — for example if we open a webshop or start collecting emails for a newsletter — we will update this page and revise the effective date above before doing so.
